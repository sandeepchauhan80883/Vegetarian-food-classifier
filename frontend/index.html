VegSnap

Point your camera.
Know your dish.

Upload a photo of a plate and this model names the dish, its meal category, and how sure it is. It's a ResNet50 backbone, fine-tuned to recognise 20 vegetarian dishes across four meal categories -- the green mark is the same one you'd find stamped on vegetarian food packaging.
